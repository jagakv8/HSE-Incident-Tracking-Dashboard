## Project Overview

The **HSE Incident Tracking & Safety Performance Dashboard** is a data analytics project designed to monitor, analyze, and visualize Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE) performance across a simulated industrial workplace.

The project focuses on transforming structured workplace safety data into meaningful insights through interactive dashboards, KPI monitoring, incident analysis, and environmental performance reporting.

The dashboard aims to help organizations identify workplace safety trends, monitor corrective actions, evaluate safety performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

**Project Type:** Personal Portfolio Project  
**Domain:** Health, Safety & Environment (HSE)  
**Reporting Period:** 2025 (Simulated Dataset)  
**Project Status:** In Progress

---

## Objectives

- Develop an interactive dashboard for monitoring workplace safety performance.

## Dashboard Modules

### 1. Safety Performance Overview

Provides a high-level overview of workplace safety performance.

Key indicators:
- Total number of incidents
- Total near misses
- Recordable injury cases
- Lost Time Injury (LTI) cases
- Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR)
- Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate (LTIFR)
- Monthly incident trends

### 2. Incident Analysis

Analyzes incidents to identify recurring patterns and potential risk areas.

Key analysis:
- Department-wise incident distribution
- Incident type classification
- Root-cause analysis
- Incident severity breakdown
- Location-wise incident trends
- Monthly incident comparisons

### 3. Safety Compliance & Corrective Actions

Monitors workplace safety improvement activities.

Key indicators:
- Total corrective actions
- Completed corrective actions
- Pending corrective actions
- Overdue corrective actions
- Corrective action closure rate
- Employee safety training completion rate

### 4. Environmental Performance

Tracks environmental performance indicators aligned with ISO 14001 principles.

Key indicators:
- Monthly electricity consumption (kWh)
- Water consumption (m³)
- Total waste generated (kg)
- Recycled waste (kg)
- Landfill waste (kg)
- Waste recycling percentage

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Calculation |
|---|---|
| TRIR | (Recordable Cases × 200,000) / Total Hours Worked |
| LTIFR | (Lost Time Cases × 1,000,000) / Total Hours Worked |
| Corrective Action Closure Rate | (Closed Actions / Total Actions) × 100 |
| Training Completion Rate | (Completed Training / Total Assigned Training) × 100 |
| Waste Recycling Rate | (Recycled Waste / Total Waste) × 100 |

*Note: TRIR and LTIFR multipliers are example reporting conventions. Actual organizational definitions and reporting requirements may differ.*

## Methodology

The project follows a structured data analytics workflow:

1. **Data Collection:** Prepare simulated workplace HSE datasets.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Use Excel and Power Query to clean and organize records.
3. **Data Transformation:** Structure incident, corrective action, training and environmental data.
4. **Data Modeling:** Establish relationships between relevant datasets in Power BI.
5. **KPI Development:** Create DAX measures to calculate safety performance indicators.
6. **Dashboard Development:** Build interactive reports and visualizations.
7. **Performance Analysis:** Identify trends, recurring causes and improvement opportunities.
